Social Media Freelancer Summit 2026 — Production Brief

Full two-day filming production covering stage capture, event coverage, and a highlight movie for the Social Media Freelancer Summit by StudentLife Academy.

Dates 05 & 06 September 2026
Hours 10:00 – 17:30

Production Scope

What we're delivering

Three camera operators across two full days, capturing the summit from every essential angle: the main stage, a dynamic side perspective, and a roaming unit covering the full energy of the event.

CAM 01
Stage Camera — Main
Fixed primary angle covering the stage and speaker in full. Clean, locked-off framing that anchors the entire edit. The main camera will be connected directly to the sound console for clean audio capture, with a simultaneous backup recording to SD card.
CAM 02
Stage Camera — Side Angle
A complementary stage angle providing coverage variety, reaction shots, and visual rhythm across the final cut.
CAM 03
Free Camera — Event Coverage
Roaming the venue capturing the stage, guests, and atmosphere. Primary unit for the summit's Highlight Movie.
🎬
Summit Highlight Movie — up to 1 min 30 sec
The free camera doubles as the Highlight Movie unit, gathering the energy, emotion, and key moments of both days. The final cut will be up to 1 minute and 30 seconds, a shareable, high-impact asset ready for distribution across social media after the event. The footage captured will also be used to create teaser content for the 2027 Summit.
