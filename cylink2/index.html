01

The mark

CyLink

Read as Cy · Link

The name is two parts — Cy for Cyprus and Link for the relationship the firm builds — set in Baskervville with the service line locked under "Link". The tittle over the i is the one warm note: Minaret Brass, the single accent, carried inside the mark itself.

02

Colour

Salt Lake palette

Drawn from the salt lake

Every colour is sampled from the Larnaka salt lake and the Hala Sultan Tekke. A dark olive green-grey anchors the brand; a moss-olive carries headings; a water blue and pale sky lift it; and a brass from the stonework is the single accent, held small.

Type

Two typefaces

Baskervville & Questrial

A classic English serif for the voice of the brand, and a minimal geometric sans for the detail. Baskervville carries the name, headlines and display; Questrial handles body, labels, tables and the service line.
