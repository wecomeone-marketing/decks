A note before we begin
Six and a half weeks is a real runway, not a comfortable one. To deliver a five-stage celestial brand world, content series, sponsorship-grade marketing, paid campaigns, and on-day multi-team coverage — every section of this meeting must produce a decision, not a discussion. Today's job is to lock scope, ownership, and timeline so production can start the same week. Items marked DECIDE TODAY cannot be deferred without compressing downstream work.

Agenda

01Introductions & Context
10 min
Quick alignment on who we are, who they are, and what success on June 13 looks like.
  • WeComeOne approach — boutique, integrated, sponsorship-aware
  • Organizer background, motivation, vision for Edition 01 and the annual ambition
  • What "a successful event" looks like — turnout, sponsor satisfaction, press coverage, or a specific combination
02Event Discovery — Locking the Facts
15 min
Get the inputs we need to scope. Anything missing here delays everything downstream.
  • Working name, tagline, positioning
  • Confirmed: Laiki Geitonia · June 13 · free entry · 5 stages · art expos · 4–6 hour duration
  • Event hours — exact start and end time (likely early-evening to late-night, factoring sunset ~8pm)
  • Programming model — do all 5 stages run concurrently for the full window, or are peaks staggered? Staggered programming spreads coverage richness and audience flow between corners
  • Stage breakdown — confirmed genres, exact stage corners, capacity per stage
  • Lighting plan per stage — whose responsibility, what's planned. Critical for night content quality. Not our scope to deliver, but directly affects the visual standard we can promise
  • Lineup status — confirmed artists vs. in-discussion, per stage
  • Art expos — number of artists, format (installations, live painting, exhibitions), curation lead, daylight vs. lit display window
  • Bar co-hosts — confirm all 5–6 are aligned, what's been agreed in-kind or financially
  • Audience expectations and demographic skew per stage
  • Marketing budget bracket
  • Ownership boundaries — organizer handles permits, licensing, artist contracts, logistics; we handle marketing end-to-end
Decide todayBudget bracket and ownership boundaries. Without these, scoping is guesswork.
03Brand Foundations — A Celestial Universe
10 min
Light-touch identity built around the celestial concept, designed to grow year-on-year.
  • Master event name, lockup, tagline
  • Logo direction — potentially a constellation arrangement of the 5 stages
  • Color palette and typography
  • Five stage sub-brands — each carrying a celestial name (planets, constellations, moons, or stars), paired with its genre, with its own color and symbol within the master system
  • Star-map treatment for the Laiki Geitonia neighborhood map
  • Edition system for annual continuity (Edition 01 / 2026)
Decide todayCelestial system family (planets / constellations / moons / stars). Brand work cannot start without it, and brand work is the gating dependency for everything else.
04Digital Presence Setup
5 min
Where the audience finds the event and where paid traffic lands.
  • Instagram, TikTok, Facebook account creation, handles, bios
  • Linktree or single-page hub (Carrd / Notion) — lineup, stage map, host bars, FAQs, accessibility
  • Meta Pixel and UTM structure for paid tracking
  • Hashtag strategy — master event tag plus per-stage tags
05Content Strategy & Production Plan
15 min
The biggest production block. Volume must match the runway — be honest about what fits.

Pre-event teasers

  • Concept reveal and stage announcement waves (paced or simultaneous)
  • Organizer intro reel
  • Artist invitation reels per stage — reggae, jazz, rock and others to be confirmed
  • "Meet the Neighborhood" series featuring each host bar paired with its stage
  • Behind-the-scenes from stage builds and rehearsals
  • Countdown sequence in the final 10 days

On the day

  • Multi-team roving plan across 5 stages and expo zones, scaled to the 4–6 hour window — coverage is concentrated, not extended
  • Shot planning across phases — golden hour openers, blue hour transition, full-night peak energy
  • Video — master highlight reel, per-stage cutdowns, social-first vertical edits, sponsor cutdowns
  • Photo — editorial, crowd, artist portraits, sponsor activations, expo documentation
  • Live story posting cadence and UGC repost workflow, staffed for a compressed activity spike

Post-event

  • Recap reel (master plus per-stage edits), photo galleries, sponsor reports
  • Press release and "Save the date — Edition 02" tease
Reality checkConfirm artist availability for invitation reels by end of week 1 — these need to start filming immediately. If most artists aren't yet confirmed, the teaser strategy shifts to atmosphere-led content rather than artist-led.
06Print & On-Site Collateral
5 min
Everything physical. Long lead times — print suppliers don't bend.
  • Posters and flyers for the neighborhood and partner venues
  • Social tiles per stage
  • Stage signage, backdrops, and host-bar window decals ("[Bar Name] · home of the Lyra Stage")
  • Neighborhood wayfinding — printed star-map of Laiki Geitonia with the 5 stages
  • Sponsor walls, expo signage, lanyards for staff and artists
Hard deadlineAll print files must lock 10–14 days before June 13. Sponsor logos must be finalized before that. This works backwards into sponsorship close dates.
07Paid Promotion
10 min
Reach beyond organic. Segmented by stage genre to hit distinct music communities.
  • Meta and TikTok ad waves — announcement, lineup, final push
  • Creative segmentation — separate ad sets per stage genre, separate audiences
  • Targeting: Larnaka core plus Nicosia / Limassol day-trip radius, interest layers per genre
  • Retargeting from teaser engagers and Linktree visitors
  • Budget recommendation tied to objectives (awareness vs. attendance vs. content amplification)
08PR & Influencer / Creator Seeding
10 min
Earned reach. Cyprus is small — relationships and timing matter more than volume.
  • Press release plan and Cyprus media targets — Politis, Phileleftheros, In-Cyprus, City Free Press, Time Out Cyprus, event listing sites
  • Radio partnerships — MAD, Mix FM, genre-specific shows
  • Influencer / creator seeding — tiered list by genre fit (lifestyle, music, Larnaka-local, art & culture)
  • Comp access, content briefs, on-day hosting roles
09Sponsorship Strategy — The Commercial Engine
15 min
Free event = sponsors fund it. This is the most commercially important block.
  • Tier framework: Title / Stage Sponsors (one per celestial stage) / Activation Partners / Supporting Partners
  • Per-tier inclusions: logo placement, social mentions, on-stage moments, branded content, hospitality, exclusivity
  • Stage sponsorship as hero offering — each celestial stage carries a sponsor's name (e.g., "Lyra Stage, presented by [Sponsor]")
  • Sponsor activation deliverables in scope — branded content production, on-site activation creative, custom social posts, post-event sponsor reports with reach, impressions, UGC metrics
  • Annual vision as a sponsor selling point — "join Edition 01, lock in Edition 02 priority"
  • Decide: does the organizer have sponsors lined up, or does WeComeOne build the deck and co-pitch?
Decide todayWhether WeComeOne builds the sponsorship deck. If yes, this becomes week-one work and reshapes the timeline. If no, confirm who is selling and to what timeline.
09bBar Co-Host Structure — Laiki Geitonia
5 min
5–6 bars, one per stage. Co-hosts, not sponsors. Infrastructure, atmosphere, amplification.
  • Confirm the existing arrangement with each bar — what's already agreed
  • Each bar paired with a celestial stage and its genre — strong narrative hook
  • Co-marketing toolkit — pre-made stories, posts, captions in GR/EN so each bar amplifies effortlessly to its own following
  • "Meet the Neighborhood" content series — one reel per bar, paced across the pre-event window, featuring the bar's character, its paired stage, and the owner / staff
  • Inclusion across all collateral — stage map, social, print, signage outside each venue
Risk flagBars are now infrastructure, not optional partners. If one drops out late, it's a stage problem. Confirm all 5–6 are committed before brand work prints their names.
10Weather & Crisis Contingency Comms
3 min
Outdoor event in Cyprus in June. Heat, wind, the unexpected.
  • Pre-drafted templates for postponement, partial cancellation, heat advisories
  • Owned channels primed for fast deployment
  • Decision tree — who calls it, when, how it's communicated

5 min

  • Paid ads launch by week 3 to give campaigns time to optimize

5 min

The frame to set in the meeting
For a free event, every marketing decision is also a sponsor-acquisition decision. Sponsors don't pay for events — they pay for audience and association. The better the marketing looks, the easier the sponsorship sell. WeComeOne is positioned here as a full marketing partner, not a vendor — which is what 5 stages, 5 host bars, a celestial brand world, and a 6.5-week runway actually require.
