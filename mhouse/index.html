Tone & feel

Four surfaces.
One language.

The website moves between four background surfaces. Each has one specific job. Together they create a consistent rhythm that feels natural and considered — not designed.

Cream — most of the site
Bright, airy. Photography leads.
The default surface. Used for rooms, coworking, contact, and general content. Generous spacing. Serif headings. Photography fills frames. Nothing competes with the images.
Dark wood — key moments
The hero.
The footer.
Used for the hero section, footer, and occasionally a full-width panel between content sections. Warm, not cold. Grounds the page without dominating it.
Sand — warmth and texture
Subtle section breaks and testimonial panels.
Used sparingly for secondary content blocks, quote sections, and visual breaks. Adds warmth and texture without introducing noise.
Teal — the garden energy
Events.
The outdoors.
Reserved for events and courtyard sections. Brings the living garden into the website. Used as a full panel background — never scattered throughout the page.
Design principles

What this
site stands for.

We will avoid
  • — Gradients of any kind
  • — Cluttered layouts or excessive copy
  • — Generic stock photography
  • — Mint used more than once
  • — Heavy shadows or decorative borders
  • — Anything that feels like a hotel template
We will prioritise
  • — Photography that fills the frame
  • — Generous cream space to breathe
  • — Teal used with confidence and restraint
  • — Mint saved for one visual reward
  • — Simple, direct booking flows
  • — A site that feels exactly like the space
