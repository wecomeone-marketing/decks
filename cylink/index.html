Overview

Three directions

Three distinct answers to the same brief, shown side by side. Each is a complete identity in its own right rather than a variation — the client chooses one to take forward.

Direction A

Corporate & Authoritative

The Nameplate — a serif masthead in the register of a printed statutory report. Authority inherited.

Direction B

Clean & Modern

The Halves — the name's own seam, Cy and link, shown in tone alone. Precision made visible.

Direction C

Approachable & Professional

The Wide — light geometric letters stretched across the measure. Presence without weight.

Direction A

Corporate & Authoritative

For the client who wants Cylink to look like it has been signing opinions for forty years.

Rationale

The Nameplate

Set as a masthead — name above, services below, a hairline holding the two together. The rule is the link: the same hairline that separates a column heading from its figures in a set of accounts. Authority here comes from what has been left out.

Direction C

Approachable & Professional

For the client who wants Cylink to look like the firm that actually picks up the phone.

Rationale

The Wide

Light letters stretched across the full measure, with the services sitting quietly beneath. Width replaces weight as the source of presence — the mark takes up room without raising its voice, which is a more accurate picture of a partner-led firm than anything heavier would be.
