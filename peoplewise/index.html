We hear what you're building

Content Direction

What People Wise talks about

The content strategy is built around five thematic pillars that align directly with Anastasia's areas of expertise and her audience's interests.

The Coaching Perspective

Thought leadership on what modern coaching looks like, dispelling myths and positioning People Wise as the progressive alternative to traditional HR.

Mental Health at Work

Practical, evidence-based insights on burnout, stress, psychological safety, and building workplaces where people genuinely thrive.

Know Yourself

Self-awareness content: exercises, reflections, and challenges that invite the audience to engage actively and share. High-engagement, highly shareable.

Emotional Intelligence in Practice

Real-world EQ application for leaders, managers, and professionals. Stories, case studies, and frameworks made accessible and actionable.

The Art of It

Expressive arts as a coaching tool: introducing audiences to this distinctive approach through curiosity-driven content. This pillar is People Wise's signature differentiator.

Instagram

The visual and emotional home of People Wise. Carousel posts, Reels, reflective captions, and Stories that build a community of individuals invested in their own growth. Primary audience: professionals aged 28 to 45.

LinkedIn

The professional authority channel. Long-form articles, thought leadership posts, and corporate outreach that positions Anastasia as the go-to expert for organisations looking to invest in their people. Primary audience: HR managers, CEOs, and L&D leaders.

Website Blog

SEO-optimised articles that build long-term organic search visibility and serve as the depth layer behind every social post. A library that grows in value over time.
