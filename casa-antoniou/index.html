01

A house with a story

Long before it welcomes its first guest, Casa Antoniou has already lived a full life.

The walls have watched a family grow, the courtyard has held gatherings we will never know the details of, and the stone underfoot has been walked by generations of Larnaca. Restoring a house like this is not renovation. It is an act of care, and it deserves a brand, a website and a social presence built with the same patience and attention that went into the restoration itself.

We have spent time thinking about the building, its rooms, its light and its history, and we can see clearly what Casa Antoniou is becoming: not simply a place to sleep in Larnaca, but a place people choose, return to, and tell their friends about long after they have checked out.

02

Beyond a place to stay

Larnaca has plenty of hotels. It has very few destinations.

Casa Antoniou has the rare advantage of real character: a restored old house with a history that no new build can manufacture, set in a city that guests are only beginning to discover beyond its airport and its seafront.

The opportunity is to build Casa Antoniou as an experience first and a hotel second. Every touchpoint, from the first photograph a guest sees online to the linen on their bed, should say the same thing: this is a house with a past, cared for by people who understand its value, and open now for guests to be part of its next chapter.

Done well, this turns Casa Antoniou into the kind of place people plan a trip around, rather than book on the way to somewhere else.

03

Scope of work

We see branding, the website and social media as one connected system, not three separate jobs. Each pillar below is built to support the other two.

Brand identity

A brand for Casa Antoniou that carries the warmth and craftsmanship of the restoration into every material a guest touches, on screen and in hand.

  • Refinement and file preparation of your existing logo for digital, print and signage use
  • A colour palette and typography system drawn from the building itself
  • A brand guidelines document covering voice, tone, usage, and clear dos and don'ts
  • Key visuals and art direction for photography and video
  • Collateral including stationery, signage, and in room and print touchpoints

Website

A refined, mobile first website that reads like a beautifully written guidebook to the property, built to turn lookers into bookers.

  • A multi page brochure website covering Home, Stay, Dining, Events and Contact
  • Direct booking on the site through a dedicated booking platform, integrated so guests see live availability, instant booking, and secure online card payment
  • A dedicated Events section for private and corporate events, where a guest can choose a package, add services such as photography, videography, catering and drinks, styling and decor, and entertainment, and send an enquiry for a bespoke proposal, with no fixed prices shown
  • Full hosting, setup and launch
  • A fast, elegant site built around one goal: guests who look, book

Social media management

A social presence that shows Casa Antoniou living and breathing between visits, and gives every follower a reason to plan their own stay.

  • Channel strategy for Instagram and Facebook to start
  • Content pillars and a monthly content calendar
  • Direction for photography and short form video, including reels and stories
  • Community management and enquiry handling
  • Paid campaigns, including click to WhatsApp traffic campaigns for rooms and events
  • Monthly reporting
04

Our approach

We move through six phases, from understanding the building to supporting the brand long after launch.

1

Discovery

We spend time on site, review the building's history, and set clear goals for the brand and the website.

2

Brand

We refine your existing brand identity, palette, typography and guidelines, testing them against real materials the property will actually use.

3

Design

We design the website experience page by page, from Home through to the Events enquiry flow.

4

Build

We build the site, connect booking and payments, and test everything end to end.

5

Launch

We launch the brand and the site together, and open the social channels with a content calendar ready to go.

6

Ongoing support

We manage social media month to month, keep the site running smoothly, and report on what is working.
