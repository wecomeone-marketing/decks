About the show

A table where real conversations happen.

RoundUp Sessions is a professionally produced roundtable talk show based in Cyprus. Each episode brings together five guests from the same industry for an open, honest and unscripted conversation. Produced and distributed across five major platforms, each episode generates organic reach, editorial content and genuine moments that audiences actually watch.

The Format
Five guests. One topic. No scripts. Each episode is a long-form roundtable conversation hosted in a professionally styled studio environment, with a multi-camera setup, broadcast-quality sound and natural lighting.
The Distribution
Every episode is cut into 15 to 20 short-form pieces distributed across YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and LinkedIn, giving each conversation a long tail of visibility well beyond the release date.
The Audience
Professionals, creatives, entrepreneurs and curious people across Cyprus and the wider region. Over 85% of Instagram viewers are non-followers, meaning the show consistently reaches new audiences, not just existing fans.
