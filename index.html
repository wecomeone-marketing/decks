A note on delivery
For a free festival, every marketing decision is also a sponsor and partner decision. We've structured the engagement so that the organic content phase (May 18 → June 5) builds the warm audience, partner venues amplify through their own channels, and the concentrated paid campaign in the final week harvests that audience for after-party ticket conversions. Both phases must land for the strategy to deliver — and we've sized the team and timeline to make that achievable within the runway.

We're looking forward to building Edition 01 with you and laying the groundwork for what becomes Larnaka's signature annual arts and music event.
