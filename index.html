Four ideas

Each one built around your brands.

01 Episode Sponsorship

The "Driven by" Episode Partnership

Sponsor a specific episode where the audience naturally overlaps with your customer. The Entrepreneurs episode positions BMW or Land Rover as the "Official Mobility Partner" — no hard sell, just a brand that belongs next to successful people. The Advertising Agency Owners episode is a natural fit for MINI — creative professionals, urban lifestyle, exactly that audience.

What you get "Driven by [Brand]" credit across all episode assets. Logo in 14–18 short-form clips. 10-day paid campaign. ~1.3M impression reach.
02 Value Exchange

The Test Drive Giveaway

No cash required. Pilakoutas provides a weekend test drive experience — a BMW X3, Land Rover Defender, or any model that fits — as a RoundUp giveaway prize. One competition post, one weekend of a demonstrator vehicle, your car in front of 1.3 million impressions of aspirational professionals who are exactly your buyers.

What you get Dedicated giveaway post and story set across all RoundUp channels. Brand tagged across the full campaign. Zero cash outlay — product only.
03 Co-Created Content

Drive & Discuss

A short-form branded content series that sits alongside RoundUp — not replacing it. Each episode, a guest is driven to the recording location in a Pilakoutas vehicle. The conversation starts in the car. 60 to 90 seconds, shot handheld, raw and real. Distributed across RoundUp's channels and yours. This is branded content that feels nothing like an ad — because it isn't one.

What you get One co-produced short-form clip per episode. Distributed across both brands' channels. Your vehicles on screen in an editorial, aspirational context.
04 Media Partnership

CSR & Community Amplification

Pilakoutas Group runs community and sustainability initiatives that deserve more than a press release. RoundUp Sessions can act as a media partner for those moments — covering, amplifying, and distributing content around events that matter to your brand's values. This gives you editorial credibility alongside advertising presence, a combination that's very hard to buy.

What you get Content coverage of CSR events across all RoundUp platforms. Branded as a media partnership — not an ad. Reaches audiences that traditional automotive campaigns don't.
