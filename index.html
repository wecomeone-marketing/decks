Scope of Work

What we are delivering

This quotation covers two core services: brand identity and a bespoke website. Together they form the complete digital and visual foundation of People Wise.

🎨
Brand Identity
Visual language for People Wise
Logo Design Primary logo, secondary mark, and usage guidelines. Up to 3 initial concepts with 2 rounds of revisions.
Colour Palette A curated palette that reflects the holistic, human-centred philosophy of People Wise, delivered with primary, secondary, and neutral tones.
Typography System Display and body typeface pairing with hierarchy rules for consistent use across all materials.
Brand Guidelines Document A concise PDF guide covering logo usage, colours, typography, and tone of voice basics.
Social Media Kit Profile images, cover photos, and post template in brand for Instagram and LinkedIn.
🌐
Website
5-page bespoke build
5 Core Pages Home, About, Services, Contact, and one additional page (e.g. Coaching or FAQ). All pages designed and developed to brand.
Copywriting Professional web copy written for all 5 pages, aligned with Anastasia's voice and positioned for her target audience.
SEO Foundations On-page SEO setup: meta titles, descriptions, heading structure, alt text, and Google Search Console connection.
Contact and Enquiry Form A functional enquiry form connected to Anastasia's email, with confirmation response.
Mobile Responsive Fully optimised for desktop, tablet, and mobile. Includes basic speed and accessibility checks before launch.
