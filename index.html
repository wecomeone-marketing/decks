02 — WHO WE'RE TALKING TO

Three segments, one campaign

Prioritised by ease of reach and speed to a lead. The campaign creative speaks primarily to Segment A — they're the volume engine and the easiest to reach on Meta.

Segment A · Priority

Solo & small hosts

1–3 listings, often alongside a day job. Feels every late-night message personally. Price sensitive, reached on social, converts on a free guide or demo.

Segment B

Property managers

5–100+ listings, already using a PMS (Hostaway, Guesty, OwnerRez, Hostfully). Thinks in cost per unit and hours saved. Higher value, longer cycle.

Segment C

Boutique hotels

10–60 rooms, family run, hit hardest by the staffing crunch. Wants 24/7 service with a thin front desk. Needs reassurance and a human touch.

03 — THE MODEL

Educate → capture → inform

This is the mechanism the whole campaign runs on. The goal isn't to sell inside the ad — it's to educate enough that someone hands over their contact, so your team can inform and convert them directly.

Stage 1

Educate

The organic 27-post calendar plus the video explain the problem and the fix. No hard sell — just recognisable pain and a clear demonstration of the product working.

→
Stage 2

Capture

A single Meta lead ad per country offers a free guide and a soft callback request via an instant form. Low friction, low cost per lead, built for volume.

→
Stage 3

Inform

Leads land in a sheet or CRM and get the guide instantly, a short demo video, then a personal call or WhatsApp message from the team — this is where HostBuddy is actually sold.

💡
Why this shape fits an €800 budget: a full multi-stage paid funnel would starve every part of it at this spend. Concentrating paid budget on one lead objective per country, while organic content does the educating, is what makes €800/month workable.

05 — CREATIVE

The video: one person, talking straight to camera

Plain and direct outperforms polished for this audience — a real property owner talking to another property owner. One shot, one location, no cuts, no screen recording. Filmed vertically, ideally on a phone, which reads as more native and trustworthy on Instagram and Facebook than something over-produced.

Format

  • 9:16 vertical, ~28–30 seconds spoken
  • Natural light, quiet room, plain background
  • Phone at eye level, small tripod or propped
  • Burned-in Greek captions (most people watch muted)

Delivery notes

  • Talk to one person — "εσύ," not a crowd
  • Say it, don't perform it — imperfect > scripted-sounding
  • Look at the lens, not off-camera notes
  • One clear ask at the end, nothing more

06 — LEAD FOLLOW-UP

Where "inform" actually happens

A cheap lead that goes cold is wasted spend. This sequence is what turns a form submission into an actual HostBuddy conversation — and it costs nothing to run.

01
Instant

Deliver the guide

Automated email fulfilling the offer immediately — this keeps trust and sets up the next touch.

02
Day 2

03
Day 4–5

Personal outreach

A real WhatsApp message or call from the team — this is where HostBuddy is actually explained and sold.

07 — MEASUREMENT

Three numbers to watch weekly

Keep it simple at this budget. Review weekly, and after about a month, shift spend toward whichever country and creative deliver the best cost per lead that actually books a conversation.

Cost per lead

By country — the primary lever for reallocating the €800 budget between Greece and Cyprus.

Form completion rate

How many people who open the instant form actually submit it — an early signal on offer and targeting fit.

Leads → calls

How many captured leads actually take a call or reply to WhatsApp — the real test of lead quality.

08 — 90-DAY ROADMAP

From launch to first real data

Three phases, each about a month, moving from "get it live" to "know what works" to "put budget behind the winner."

01
Weeks 1–3

Launch

Turn on the Greek and Cypriot lead campaigns. Publish the guide and landing page. Film and launch the Greek video. Start capturing leads and learn which audiences are cheapest to convert.

02
Weeks 4–8

Learn

Add the Greece retargeting slice. Get the 3-step follow-up sequence fully running so leads are actually informed. Review cost per lead weekly and start correcting targeting and creative.

03
Weeks 9–12

Decide

Compare Greece vs. Cyprus performance and shift budget toward the stronger market. Decide on a Cyprus creative refresh. Set the next quarter's target based on real cost-per-lead data.
