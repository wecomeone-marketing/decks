Situation Overview
Starting Point
New ownership. Strong location. One of the most desirable brand portfolios in European motorcycling. The task now is to build the identity and infrastructure that matches the product.
Brand Portfolio
Piaggio · Vespa · Moto Guzzi · Aprilia four of Europe's most recognised and aspirational motorcycle and scooter brands under one roof
Current Status
No brand identity · No website · Weak social media presence · No professional photography · Zero Google discoverability
Opportunity
Cyprus's motorcycle market is digitally underserved. A strong brand, a cinematic website, and consistent social presence creates category ownership fast
Target Audience
Males 25–50, lifestyle-driven commuters and enthusiasts, island-wide · Secondary: tourists and expats interested in Vespa or scooter acquisition
Geographic Reach
Walk-in priority: Larnaka · Digital reach: All of Cyprus Nicosia, Limassol, Paphos, Famagusta
Phased Approach
Brand identity first then website built on that identity then social campaigns that drive traffic to both. Each phase reinforces the next
Service 01 Once-Off
BRAND IDENTITY
Before a website is built or a single post goes live, Moto Italia needs a visual identity that holds its own alongside Aprilia, Vespa, and Moto Guzzi. This is that foundation.
What's Included
Logo Design 3 Concepts
Three distinct directions presented. Client selects one for full development. Each concept delivered with rationale and brand thinking behind it
Colour Palette
Primary and secondary colours defined with HEX, RGB, and CMYK values ready for digital and print use
Typography System
Primary and secondary typefaces selected and specified for headings, body copy, and UI consistent across web and print
Logo Variations
Primary logo, horizontal version, icon/mark only, and reversed (white on dark) all formats covered
Brand Guidelines Document
Single-page or short PDF covering correct usage, spacing, colour application, and what not to do handed to any future supplier
Final File Delivery
AI, EPS, SVG, PNG, and PDF formats print and digital ready. Client owns all files outright

Why this comes first
Building a website before the brand identity is defined means rebuilding it later. Getting the logo, colours, and fonts locked in Week 1 means every deliverable after website, social templates, signage, ads is consistent from day one. This is not optional groundwork. It is the most important thing we do first.
