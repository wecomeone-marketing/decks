Four ideas

Built around the next two episodes.

01 Episode Sponsorship

Episode 1 — Entrepreneurs

The next RoundUp episode brings together five entrepreneurs from across Cyprus for an open, unscripted conversation about building businesses. Foody sponsors this episode as the "Official Delivery Partner" — a brand that's part of every entrepreneur's working day, whether they're ordering lunch at the office or feeding their team on a deadline. The association is natural, credible and completely unforced.

What you get "Powered by Foody" credit across all episode assets. Logo in 14–18 short-form clips across Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook and LinkedIn. 10-day paid campaign post-release. Reach to RoundUp's combined ~1.3M impression audience.
02 Episode Sponsorship

Episode 2 — Advertising Agency Owners

The second episode features advertising agency owners and senior creatives — the people who build campaigns for a living. These are exactly the professionals who make decisions about what platforms, brands and content their clients associate with. Sponsoring this episode puts Foody in front of the people who shape brand perception across Cyprus.

What you get "Powered by Foody" credit across all episode assets. Logo in 14–18 short-form clips across all platforms. 10-day paid campaign post-release. Foody visible to an audience of senior marketing and creative decision-makers.
03 Co-Created Content

"What Are You Ordering Tonight?"

A lightweight short-form series running across both episodes. One casual question asked to every guest before or after filming: "What are you ordering tonight?" Unscripted, human, completely on-brand for Foody. Each clip runs 20–30 seconds, features a recognisable Cyprus entrepreneur or agency owner, and ends with a Foody tag. Cheap to produce, genuinely watchable, shareable on both brands' channels.

What you get One co-produced short-form clip per episode — two clips total across the Entrepreneurs and Advertising Agency Owners episodes. Distributed across RoundUp's channels and Foody's own social platforms.
04 Audience Swap

Platform Cross-Promotion

Foody's reach in Cyprus is enormous — app notifications, social channels, in-app banners, blog content. RoundUp's reach is 1.3 million impressions across five platforms. Tie the cross-promotion to the two upcoming episodes: RoundUp promotes Foody to its growing professional audience, and Foody promotes the Entrepreneurs and Advertising Agency Owners episodes to its massive Cyprus user base. Both brands reach audiences they wouldn't otherwise. No cash changes hands.

What you get RoundUp promotes Foody across all five platforms tied to both episode releases. Foody promotes the Entrepreneurs and Advertising Agency Owners episodes via app notifications, social channels and in-platform content. Agreed assets, agreed timeline, written arrangement.
