Who We're Talking To

Audience definition

Four Core Audiences

Lavita belongs to
more than one kind of person.

The dual day/night concept unlocks four distinct audience segments. Each has different motivations, different platforms, and different messages that resonate. Smart marketing speaks to each of them precisely — not with four separate identities, but with one brand that contains multitudes.

RL

The Remote Local

28 to 42 — Larnaca resident

  • Works from cafes regularly
  • Cares about wifi, ambience, coffee quality
  • Becomes a loyal repeat visitor if the space feels right
  • Shares workspace spots on Instagram Stories
  • Active weekday mornings to afternoons
VS

The Visiting Expat

30 to 55 — Tourist or short-stay visitor

  • Discovers via Google Maps and TripAdvisor
  • Seeking "authentic" and "local" experiences
  • Heritage architecture triggers stop decisions
  • Likely to post a review after a good experience
  • Drives weekend revenue significantly
SG

The Social Group

24 to 38 — Friends, colleagues, couples

  • Plans evenings in advance or spontaneously
  • Influenced by Instagram Reels and TikTok
  • Venue atmosphere matters as much as menu
  • Drives weekday evening and weekend night revenue
  • High UGC potential if the experience delivers
CD

The Corporate Daytime

28 to 50 — Business meetings, client lunches

  • Needs reliable wifi and a calm setting
  • Will bring clients and colleagues for food
  • Responds to LinkedIn presence and Google
  • Values proximity to the city centre
  • Untapped — a natural fit for Lavita's daytime offer

The Strategic Direction

Content pillars and brand voice

Strategic Positioning

Own "the space that feels like home
but looks like a destination."

"Lavita began with a feeling. We wanted to create the kind of place we would love to spend time in." — Zack & Roxane

The founders' own words are the strategy. Marketing for Lavita should feel like an invitation, not a promotion. Every post, caption, and story should make someone feel that missing this place would be a genuine loss. The brand voice is warm, unhurried, and quietly confident. Never shouts. Never discounts. Never begs for attention.

Content Pillars
Pillar 01

The Space Tells a Story

The 1940s building is marketing that cannot be bought. Heritage details, architectural corners, the garden, the light at different times of day — show the space in ways that make people want to sit inside it.

Interior photography Reels — ambience Mood stories
Pillar 02

Two Worlds, One Address

The day-to-night shift is a unique narrative hook. Morning light with specialty coffee. The same room at 9pm with candlelight and cocktails. This contrast is scroll-stopping when executed visually.

Before/after content Timelapses Dual-mood campaigns
Pillar 03

Craft on the Table

Food and drink content that earns its place in a feed. Not the standard overhead shot — close, textured, human. A barista's hands, a cocktail mid-pour, the first bite of a shared plate.

Hero dish content Cocktail creation reels Menu season launches
Pillar 04

The People Behind It

Zack and Roxane's founding story is an emotional asset. The team, the regulars, the faces of Lavita — humanising the brand builds loyalty that no ad spend can replicate.

Founder content Team spotlights Community moments

Six-Month Roadmap

Phase by phase plan

Three Phases

Build, amplify, own the room.

Six months structured in three distinct phases. Each phase builds on the one before it. We do not rush to paid media before the organic foundation is strong enough to amplify.

M1–M2 Foundation

Set the Stage

  • Full brand audit and photography brief
  • Planned content shoot — day scenes and night scenes
  • Instagram and TikTok content rhythm established
  • Google Business Profile fully optimised
  • TripAdvisor listing optimised, review strategy activated
  • Bio links and highlights restructured
  • Content calendar — 4 posts per week minimum
  • Reel series concept developed and launched
  • Website SEO audit and on-page improvements
  • Staff briefed on UGC and review encouragement
M3–M4 Amplification

Build Momentum

  • First paid social campaign — Lavita By Night
  • Influencer / content creator collaboration (local)
  • Seasonal campaign — late summer/early autumn offer
  • Email / WhatsApp list building campaign
  • Reservation-linked CTA push on all channels
  • Retargeting audience built via Meta pixel
  • Google Maps review milestone push
  • Cross-promote with nearby Larnaca businesses
  • Monthly performance review and content pivots
  • LinkedIn activation — target corporate daytime crowd
M5–M6 Ownership

Command the Space

  • Holiday and festive season campaign
  • Group bookings and events push
  • Loyalty / return-visit incentive mechanics
  • Website blog / journal activated for SEO depth
  • PR outreach — Cyprus lifestyle press and travel media
  • Second content shoot — winter mood and festive
  • 6-month results review and Q1 2027 planning
  • Community event concept explored (pop-up, live music)
  • TikTok growth push — viral format testing
  • Paid retargeting scaled based on M3/M4 learnings

Channel Strategy

Where and how we show up

Platform by Platform

Every channel has a job to do.

We don't spread thin across every platform. Each channel is assigned a primary role and held accountable to it. Resources follow the platforms where Lavita's audiences actually live.

Instagram Primary

Brand home. The main stage for Lavita's visual identity, mood, and story. Reels drive discovery; Stories drive daily connection; grid maintains brand coherence.

  • 4+ posts per week including 2 Reels
  • Daily Stories — behind-scenes and daily specials
  • Highlights: The Space / Food & Drink / Events / Reviews
  • Paid ads when seasonal campaigns launch
TikTok Primary

Discovery and reach. TikTok's algorithm rewards novelty — Lavita's architecture and mood-shift concept are tailor-made for the platform's viral content patterns.

  • 3+ short-form videos per week
  • Trending audio applied to venue content
  • Day-to-night transformation content
  • Seasonal food/drink hero content
Google Business Primary

The single most important touchpoint for anyone searching "cafe Larnaca" or "restaurant near Finikoudes." Often overlooked. Cannot be ignored.

  • Complete all profile fields — menu, photos, hours
  • Weekly photo uploads to stay algorithmically fresh
  • Review response strategy — every review answered
  • Posts pushed weekly with seasonal content
Facebook Secondary

Reaches the 35+ demographic and tourist segment. Primary channel for event promotion and paid social to reach specific audience segments efficiently.

  • 2 posts per week — mirrored from Instagram
  • Events created for any special evenings
  • Paid campaigns targeting tourist visitors
TripAdvisor Secondary

Critical for tourist conversion. Visitors from abroad often commit to a venue based on TripAdvisor rankings before they land in Cyprus. Review volume matters.

  • Review encouragement card left with the bill
  • QR code to TripAdvisor profile at tables
  • Prompt satisfied guests via WhatsApp follow-up
LinkedIn Supporting

Reaches the corporate and professional segment — the daytime meeting and client lunch crowd. Founder storytelling content performs well here.

  • 1 post per week — founder story or brand moment
  • Target B2B professionals in Larnaca area
  • Position Lavita as the default meetings venue
