How we work

Then ads.

Most agencies launch ads in Month 1 with whatever content exists. We do not. Month 1 is a dedicated content shoot — a full day on-site producing photos and Reels that become the foundation for the next four months of posting and paid promotion. Content strategy

What we
actually make.

All content is produced from the Month 1 shoot and the Month 5 refresh. Nothing is outsourced to the client. Nothing relies on food bloggers. Every format below has been chosen because it works for a restaurant brand at this budget level.

Organic — Weekly

The 14-Hour Series

Short vertical videos following a cut of meat from raw to table. Early smoke, the rub, the slice. No voice-over needed. The process itself is the content. This format consistently earns saves and shares on Instagram and TikTok because it rewards curiosity.

Community — Recurring

The Challenge Wall

Every burger challenge attempt gets a dedicated post in a consistent format: the challenger, the clock, the result. A running scoreboard lives in Stories highlights. Followers start following specifically for the updates. The challenge becomes a live event people check back for.

Organic — Monthly

Burger of the Month Drop

Each monthly special is treated as a mini launch. A teaser on Monday. A reveal on Wednesday. A final push at the end of the month. Creates urgency and gives people a reason to visit specifically this month before it is gone.

Paid — Weekly

Friday Night Push

Short paid ads running Thursday evening through Saturday targeting a 15km radius around Larnaca. Retargeting people who engaged with previous posts. The question the ad answers is "where tonight?" — and the answer is smoke, fire, and a table worth booking.

Seasonal — Spring

The Lenten Vegan Window

During Orthodox fasting season — typically 40 to 50 days before Easter — a significant portion of the Cyprus population avoids meat and dairy. Barbeque Project already has vegan options. With targeted content and a small paid push during this period, the positioning is simple: bring the whole table. One person is fasting, three are not. Everyone eats well. This is a genuine opportunity that almost no BBQ restaurant in Cyprus is exploiting, and the messaging does not compromise the brand's core identity at all.

Community — Ongoing

Table Talk

Reposting the best customer photos with a tag. A curated Stories highlight titled "Tables" becomes social proof anyone can scroll before deciding where to eat. Costs nothing. Generates goodwill. Signals to new visitors that real people love this place.

Dine-in Driver

Bundle Deals That Fill Tables

Barbeque Project does not currently offer bundle deals — which means there is an untapped lever specifically for driving dine-in covers. A sharing platter for two, a weekday lunch combination, or a group booking package give people a concrete reason to come in rather than just intend to. These are not discount promotions — they are curated experiences with a clear price point that makes the decision easy. We design two or three offers, promote them through organic posts and paid ads, and use them as the primary call to action during quieter periods like midweek lunches and early evenings. The goal is to convert passive followers into people sitting at a table.
