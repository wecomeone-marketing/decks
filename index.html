Four ideas

Built specifically around the next two episodes.

01 Episode Sponsorship

Official Shopping Partner — Both Episodes

Jinius sponsors both the Entrepreneurs and Advertising Agency Owners episodes as the "Official Shopping Partner of RoundUp Sessions." The credit runs across all content assets — long-form video, short-form clips, captions, paid creatives — keeping Jinius visible to a combined audience of professionals across two distinct episode cycles. One partnership, two audiences, sustained presence.

What you get "Powered by Jinius" credit across all assets for both episodes. Logo in 28–36 short-form clips across Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook and LinkedIn. Two 10-day paid campaigns post-release. Reach to RoundUp's combined ~1.3M impression audience.
02 Product Giveaway

The Jinius Product Giveaway

Jinius provides a product — from any category on the platform — as a giveaway prize tied to each episode release. The product doesn't need to be expensive; it needs to be relevant and desirable to the episode's audience. A tech accessory for the Entrepreneurs episode, a lifestyle or fashion piece for the Agency Owners episode. Each giveaway runs as a dedicated post and story set across all RoundUp channels, driving entries, follows and app traffic directly to Jinius.

What you get Two dedicated giveaway posts and story sets — one per episode. Jinius featured across all campaign assets. Audience driven directly to your platform. Cost to Jinius: product only.
03 Co-Created Content

"What's in Your Cart?"

A short-form series built around one question asked to every RoundUp guest on both episodes: "What's the last thing you bought online?" Unscripted, casual, completely authentic. Each clip runs 20–30 seconds, ends with a Jinius tag, and is shared across both brands' channels. Five guests per episode means up to ten pieces of genuine, person-to-person content — the kind that algorithms reward and audiences actually watch.

What you get Up to 10 co-produced short-form clips across both episodes. Distributed across RoundUp's channels and Jinius's own social platforms. Branded content that feels like conversation, not advertising.
04 Audience Swap

Cross-Platform Promotion

Jinius has an established digital presence — social channels, newsletter, app notifications, a broad and active customer base across Cyprus. RoundUp has 1.3 million impressions across five platforms and a fast-growing professional audience. A structured cross-promotion tied to both episode releases means Jinius promotes RoundUp to its customers, and RoundUp promotes Jinius to its audience. Two platforms, two audiences, one agreement. No cash changes hands.

What you get RoundUp promotes Jinius across all five platforms tied to both episode releases. Jinius promotes the Entrepreneurs and Advertising Agency Owners episodes via social, newsletter and in-app content. Agreed assets, agreed timeline, written arrangement.
