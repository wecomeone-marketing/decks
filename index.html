Introduction

A space worth
telling the world about.

M House is more than a place to stay. It is a living experience — five intimate rooms housed within two beautifully restored traditional buildings in Larnaca, a lush courtyard with a swimming pool, and a thoughtfully designed coworking space. The letter M carries personal meaning, a tribute to a beloved mother — and that depth of feeling should be at the heart of every marketing decision.

Following our initial discovery meeting, Wecomeone Marketing And Comms has prepared this proposal to outline the strategic marketing framework we recommend for M House. Our goal is to establish M as Larnaca's most desirable boutique destination across three distinct revenue streams: accommodation, events, and coworking.

Market Understanding

Who we are
speaking to.

M House speaks to three distinct audiences across its three spaces. A clear understanding of each audience shapes every creative decision, channel strategy, and piece of content we produce.

Boutique Travellers

Discerning travellers; European and international; seeking authentic, character-rich experiences over mass-market hotels. They value design, story, and locality. They book through Instagram, Google, Booking.com, and word of mouth.

Event Hosts

Couples planning intimate weddings or civil ceremonies, corporates seeking venue hire for retreats and networking evenings, and private individuals celebrating life moments in an exclusive setting.

Remote Workers & Nomads

Digital nomads, freelancers, and small teams based in or visiting Larnaca seeking a premium, inspiring coworking environment; with the added appeal of a boutique hotel atmosphere.

Local Community

Larnaca residents and Cyprus-based professionals who will discover M through social media, local PR, and influencer partnerships. Often the first ambassadors and event attendees of a local gem.

Scope of Work

What we will
build together.

Our recommended scope covers seven integrated service areas. Each is designed to work both independently and as part of a cohesive, always-on marketing ecosystem for M House. Woven throughout is a broader opportunity: positioning M House not just as a venue, but as a full-service event destination; where guests can book the space and seamlessly access a curated layer of creative services and trusted supplier recommendations, all presented under the M House name.

1
Brand Identity

Branding & Visual Identity

Before anything can be built or promoted, M House needs a clear, cohesive brand identity that reflects the soul of the space; its heritage, its intimacy, and the personal meaning behind the name. A strong brand foundation ensures every touchpoint; from the website to social media to printed collateral; speaks with one consistent, recognisable voice.

  • Brand strategy workshop: defining positioning, tone of voice, and brand values
  • Logo design and refinement (primary logo, secondary marks, monogram)
  • Full brand identity system: colour palette, typography, graphic language, patterns and textures
  • Brand guidelines document for consistent application across all channels
  • Business card and stationery design
  • Signage and in-room collateral design (welcome cards, menus, branded amenities)
  • Digital brand kit: social media templates, story frames, highlight covers
2
Digital Foundation

Website Design & Development

The website is the single most important marketing asset for M House. It must communicate the soul of the space in the first five seconds, convert visitors into bookings, and function flawlessly across all devices. We will design and build a bespoke, high-performance website that reflects the elegance and character of M — and that does more than showcase the space; it actively drives revenue across all three streams.

  • Full custom website design and development (desktop, tablet & mobile)
  • Individual landing pages for Rooms, Events, and Coworking; each with dedicated booking/enquiry functionality
  • Online booking integration for room reservations (direct booking to reduce OTA commission dependency)
  • Events enquiry form with availability calendar
  • Coworking space booking or membership enquiry module
  • On-page SEO implementation: metadata, schema markup, site speed optimisation, image compression
  • Google Analytics 4 and Meta Pixel integration for full tracking capability
  • Brand-aligned visual language: typography, colour palette, photography layout, micro-animations
  • Blog/journal section for ongoing SEO content and storytelling
  • Multi-language consideration (English / Greek as a minimum)
Events Add-On Feature

Event Enhancement Packages

Within the Events section of the website, we recommend building a dedicated "Make It Memorable" module — a curated menu of optional enhancements that event clients can explore and enquire about at the point of booking. These services are presented as M House offerings, giving the venue an elevated, full-service positioning while the creative production is delivered by Wecomeone Marketing And Comms.

  • →Professional event photography packages (coverage options from 2 to 8 hours)
  • →Event videography and highlight reel production (same-day edits and cinematic cuts available)
  • →Graphic design services: custom invitations, save-the-dates, printed menus, signage, and branded event materials
  • →Preferred Supplier Network: a curated directory of vetted local suppliers (DJ, catering, floral, lighting) available on request
  • →Enquiry flow designed to capture add-on interest at the point of event booking; maximising revenue per event from day one
3
Local Visibility

Google Business Profile Setup & Optimisation

A fully optimised Google Business Profile is essential for local discovery, especially for boutique properties. When travellers search "boutique hotel Larnaca" or "event venue Larnaca", M must appear with compelling visuals, accurate information, and strong reviews. We will build this profile from the ground up and maintain it as an ongoing asset.

  • Google Business Profile creation and full verification
  • Category configuration across Hotel, Event Venue, and Coworking Space
  • Professional photography upload and gallery curation
  • Service and amenity listings for all three revenue streams
  • Business description copywriting (EN)
  • Q&A section pre-population with anticipated guest questions
  • Review generation strategy and response protocols
  • Booking link integration connecting directly to the M website
  • Monthly Google Posts schedule to keep the profile active and searchable
4
Content Creation

Photography, Videography & Visual Content

For a property of M House's calibre, visual content is not a nice-to-have; it is the core of the marketing strategy. The architecture, the courtyard, the rooms, the coworking space: all of it needs to be captured with intention. We will plan and execute a comprehensive content production programme to build a rich library of assets for use across all channels.

  • Full-day professional photography shoot: rooms, courtyard, pool, coworking space, architectural details, lifestyle
  • Brand video production: a hero film for the website capturing the soul and atmosphere of M (60–90 seconds)
  • Short-form vertical video content for Reels (series of 6–10 clips per quarter)
  • Drone aerial photography and video of the property and surrounding Larnaca setting
  • Event photography and videography: on-site coverage of hosted events to build a live showcase library and supply the Event Enhancement Package offering
  • Graphic design for events: invitations, save-the-dates, printed menus, programmes, and branded event collateral produced per booking as part of the add-on service
  • UGC (User Generated Content) programme: guidelines and incentives for guests to create and share authentic content
  • Influencer collaboration campaign: identification, briefing, and coordination of relevant local and international creators
  • Seasonal content calendar with creative direction for all three spaces
5
Organic Social

Social Media Management

M House's social media presence should feel like an invitation — aspirational but warm, curated but human. We will manage the day-to-day presence across the most relevant platforms, ensuring M is consistently visible, engaging, and on-brand. Priority platforms: Instagram and Facebook.

  • Instagram and Facebook profile set-up and brand alignment
  • Monthly content calendar: 10–12 posts per month across platforms
  • Copywriting in English for all posts
  • Stories strategy: behind-the-scenes, guest features, seasonal promotions, event teasers
  • Community management: responses to comments, DMs, and reviews
  • Hashtag research and geotagging strategy for Larnaca and Cyprus tourism
  • Monthly performance reporting with insights and recommendations
6
Paid Advertising

Meta & Google Paid Advertising

Organic content builds awareness over time — paid advertising accelerates it. We recommend a phased approach: an initial launch campaign to establish brand presence, followed by always-on campaigns targeting each of the three booking types. Budget recommendations will be provided in a separate pricing document.

  • Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram): campaign strategy, creative production, audience targeting and campaign management
  • Google Search Ads: keyword strategy targeting high-intent searches ("boutique hotel Larnaca", "event venue Larnaca", "coworking Larnaca")
  • Google Display & Remarketing: retargeting website visitors and lookalike audiences
  • Seasonal and event-specific campaign planning (summer, festive season, local events)
  • Monthly ad performance reporting: impressions, clicks, CTR, cost-per-booking, ROAS
  • A/B testing of creatives and copy to continuously improve performance
  • Ad spend recommendation per revenue stream (rooms, events, coworking); to be detailed in pricing phase
7
Additional Recommendations

PR, Listings & Reputation Management

Beyond the core services above, we recommend a set of additional initiatives that will compound the impact of the primary strategy. These can be phased in over the first 6 months to avoid overwhelm at launch.

  • OTA presence strategy: Booking.com, Airbnb, and Expedia profile optimisation with a roadmap to shift bookings toward direct (website) over time
  • PR outreach: pitching M House to Cyprus lifestyle media, travel publications, and European boutique hotel directories
  • Email marketing: welcome sequences for new guests, event follow-ups, and seasonal newsletter to a growing subscriber list
  • TripAdvisor listing setup and review strategy
  • Partnership development: identify synergies with local restaurants, tour operators, wedding planners, and corporate clients for cross-referral opportunities
  • Branded collateral design: digital welcome packs, event brochures, coworking membership information (print-ready and digital)
Strategic Roadmap

How we
get there.

We recommend a phased approach to ensure the foundations are built correctly before amplifying reach. Each phase has clear priorities and outcomes.

I
Month 1–2 · Foundation

Discovery, Strategy & Build

Deep-dive brand strategy session. Photography and video production. Website design and development. Google Business Profile creation. Social media account setup and brand alignment. Establishment of content pillars and visual identity guidelines.

II
Month 2–3 · Launch

Go Live & Initial Amplification

Website launch. Launch campaign across Meta and Google. First influencer collaborations. Organic social goes live across Instagram and Facebook. PR outreach begins. OTA profiles activated. Google Business optimisation live.

III
Month 3–6 · Growth

Ongoing Management & Optimisation

Monthly content production and social management. Always-on paid campaigns. SEO content via blog/journal. Review and reputation management. Monthly performance reporting and strategy refinement. Email list building begins.

IV
Month 6+ · Scale

Seasonal Campaigns & Direct Booking Growth

Peak season campaign planning (summer). Event venue push for Q4 festive season. Shift strategy toward increasing direct bookings vs OTA dependency. Expand content formats and partnership network. Annual review and strategy evolution.
