What Travio is

Budget-accurate, bookable trips in seconds.

Travio turns a traveller's budget, dates and style into three budget-accurate destinations and a complete, day-by-day itinerary, each built around real places and real prices.

The what: genuine trip planning, not inventory search. The how: artificial intelligence grounded in live flight, hotel and venue data. The why: travellers lose hours to uncertain planning, and travel agents lose billable time building every itinerary by hand. Travio removes both, direct to the consumer and licensed to the trade.

The problem

Planning a trip is slow, fragmented and uncertain.

For travellers

You juggle flights, hotels and things to do across a dozen tabs, and still cannot tell whether the trip fits your budget until hours of research are done. So people overspend, under-plan, or rebook the same familiar place.

For travel agents

A good multi-day itinerary is built by hand and takes hours of research per client. That caps how many clients one agent can serve and squeezes already-thin margins.

Today's tools each solve only part of it: online travel agencies sell inventory but do not plan a trip or check a budget, and generic AI invents hotels and guesses prices that are simply wrong.

How it works

Three steps, a few seconds.

1

Tell Travio the basics

Your budget, dates, group and travel style. No account needed.

2

Get three matched destinations

Each with live flight and hotel prices and a clear verdict on whether it fits your budget.

3

Open a full day-by-day itinerary

Built around real, well-rated restaurants and attractions with exact map links, then hand off to booking partners in one click.

Why it is different

Grounded, bookable, and honest about the budget.

Live prices, not estimates

Flight and hotel prices come from live Google data, so the budget reflects what the traveller will actually pay.

Real venues, not hallucinations

Restaurants and attractions are grounded in real, rated places with links to the exact map pin, the thing generic AI gets wrong.

A budget that holds together

The verdict is verified on the server and the same figures flow from the destination card into the itinerary, so nothing contradicts itself.

Seconds, not hours

A full, bookable plan appears in the time it takes to read this sentence.

The model

Sold to travellers, licensed to the trade.

The engine

B2B licensing

A per-seat subscription for licensed travel agents using Travio as an AI itinerary co-pilot, €59 per seat per month, recurring.

The shop window

B2C consumer

The free consumer app, with a premium tier and affiliate commissions, which also proves the product and feeds the agent pipeline.

A deliberate shield: the licensed agents hold the travel licences and the booking liability, so Travio stays a pure, low-regulation software layer.
