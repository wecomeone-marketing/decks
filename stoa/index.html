Sharing the cost

How the six share it.

STOA is one brand run by many doors. However you divide the cost between yourselves, here are three fair ways to split it. How we then invoice, pooled or per shop, comes next.

Equal shares

Every door pays the same monthly membership into a shared pot. Simplest, and keeps everyone aligned. Recommended.

Weighted

Doors that gain most from footfall (the F&B venues) contribute more than the spa and barber.

Anchor plus members

A lead tenant or the landlord underwrites the base, others buy in per event or per season.

Whichever split you choose, one nominated person approves budgets so decisions stay fast.
How billing works

Two ways to be invoiced.

However you split the cost, we can bill it two ways. Both put the exact same plan behind the arcade.

Option A · one pooled invoice

  • We invoice a single nominated payer once a month, and you settle your shares privately
  • Lowest admin, most reliable cash flow, one point of approval
  • Best when you can agree a lead payer or a shared STOA account

Recommended.

Option B · split per shop

  • Each shop gets its own invoice with two fixed lines: the retainer membership and a share of the monthly ad spend
  • Both lines are the same every month, so the invoice stays predictable
  • Events are added only when they run, and shop specific requests are billed to that shop alone

More admin, but every invoice stays predictable.
