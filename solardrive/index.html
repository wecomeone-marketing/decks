01
Overview

A fast, premium one page website that turns visits from villa owners, real estate developers and car dealerships into qualified leads, with a secure space where approved investors can access the business plan.

All copy, technical data and certifications are supplied by SolarDrive. Wecomeone handles design, build, hosting, domain, corporate email and launch, fully operational by 10 November 2026.

02
Scope

What is included

  • A

    Starter visual identity

    Wordmark logo, colour palette and typeface, based on the materials supplied by SolarDrive. One concept and one round of revisions.

  • B

    Landing page design and build

    Custom one page design in a clean, tech focused, premium style, including integration of the ROI calculator supplied by SolarDrive. Mobile responsive, optimised for speed, SSL, privacy notice and cookie consent.

  • C

    Lead generation forms

    Consultation form with audience selection (villa owner, real estate developer, car dealership) and a separate investor access request form. Spam protection and instant delivery to your corporate inbox.

  • D

    Investor data room

    A private Google Drive folder within your Google Workspace. Investors request access through the website, you approve each request personally and share the folder with that investor only. Access can be removed at any time.

  • E

    Domain and corporate email setup

    Domain registration in the company's name, DNS configuration, Google Workspace with one mailbox and a sales alias, email authentication (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) and branded signatures.

  • F

    Basic SEO optimisation

    Keyword focused page title, meta description and headings, image optimisation, social sharing previews, XML sitemap, and setup of Google Search Console and Google Analytics.

  • G

    Priority delivery

    Dedicated scheduling to guarantee launch by 10 November 2026.
