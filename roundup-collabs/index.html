About the show

A table where real conversations happen.

RoundUp Sessions is a professionally produced roundtable talk show based in Cyprus. Each episode brings together five guests from the same industry for an open, honest and unscripted conversation. The long-form episode runs approximately 90 minutes and is distributed across five major platforms. Every episode is also cut into 15 to 20 short-form pieces distributed across YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and LinkedIn, giving each conversation a long tail of visibility well beyond the release date.

The Format
Five guests. One topic. No scripts. Each episode is a long-form roundtable conversation in a professionally styled studio environment, with a multi-camera setup, broadcast-quality sound and natural lighting.
The Distribution
Each episode is produced as a long-form conversation of approximately 90 minutes, then cut into 15 to 20 short-form pieces distributed across YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and LinkedIn, giving each conversation a long tail of visibility well beyond the release date.
The Audience
Professionals, creatives, entrepreneurs and curious people across Cyprus and the wider region. Over 85% of Instagram viewers are non-followers, meaning the show consistently reaches new audiences.

Commission a production

We produce your roundtable from A to Z.

All you need to do is invite your guests and host the episode. We take care of everything else.

RoundUp Sessions takes full responsibility for planning, coordination, on-site execution and post-production delivery. Our goal is to remove all operational complexity from your team and deliver a broadcast-ready result predictable, professional, and ready to be presented to partners and sponsors.

Scope of Service

  • Streamlined senior production crew with 5-camera setup, professional lighting and sound
  • Production planning and full coordination
  • Make-up artist
  • Set design and visual styling (standard setup*)
  • Location scouting and studio rental**
  • Catering for hosts, guests and crew
  • Lunch for hosts and crew (full-day productions)
  • Standard roundtable furniture for up to 7 participants
  • On-site production management and supervision
  • All crew, equipment, logistics and coordination handled entirely by RoundUp Sessions

Deliverables

Long-form episode [16:9, 1080p]
Up to 90 minutes in duration. Includes intro, outro, b-roll inserts, credits, licensed music and subtitles. Delivered within 10 working days of the shooting date. Up to 2 revision rounds included.
Short-form clips [9:16, 1080p] — up to 20 clips
Extracted from the roundtable recording. Optimised for social media distribution. Includes subtitles and one teaser video of up to 20 seconds. Clip points provided by your team within an agreed timeframe.
